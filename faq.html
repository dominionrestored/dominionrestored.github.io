About the game

What is Dominion Restored?

An 8-bit action adventure that travels through the story of the Bible, from the Garden of Eden to the book of Revelation. You explore ten lands, each with a dungeon, a treasure and a boss, plus fourteen mini-games in circus tents along the way.

Which devices can I play on?

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android phones and tablets, and Windows PCs. See Get the game for the stores.

Are there ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions?

No. You buy the game once and everything in it is yours. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Is it suitable for children?

It is made for families. The combat is classic 8-bit action against creatures and Bible-story foes, with no gore. Enemies simply vanish when defeated. Check the age rating shown on your store for the official rating, and as always, parents know their children best.

Which Bible translation does the game quote?

The King James Version.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Once installed, it plays completely offline.

Is there anything after the final battle?

Yes. We will let you discover it.

Playing

How do I control the game?

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen stick to move, A to use your sword, B to use your item, ITEM to switch items and START to pause. On a Mac or PC you can use the keyboard:

How do I save?

The game saves automatically as you go: when you move to a new screen, find a treasure or defeat a boss. Choose CONTINUE on the title screen to pick up where you left off.

Can I switch between 2D and 3D?

Yes. Open OPTIONS on the title screen or in the pause menu and change VIEW. In 3D, CAMERA lets you follow the hero up close or see the whole room. The game plays the same either way.

Can I change how my hero looks?

Yes. OPTIONS has four looks to choose from, and the game remembers your choice.

What are the circus tents?

Each land has a circus tent with a mini-game from its story, and a few extra tents hold bonus games. They are open from the start, before or after you beat that land's dungeon. Once you have found a tent, you can replay its game from ARCADE on the title screen without touching your saved game.

I'm stuck. Any hints?
  • Read the signs. They often point the way.
  • Each dungeon has a locked treasure room guarded by a mini-boss. Its treasure also opens the boss door.
  • Small keys open locked doors. Clear a room of enemies to find them.
  • Some bosses can only be hurt in a particular way. Think about how the Bible story goes.
  • Your Faith meter (the blue bar) powers your items and refills over time.

Your saved game

Can I move my save to another device?

Not at this time. Your progress is stored only on the device you play on, which is part of how the game keeps your information private.

What happens to my save if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes your saved game with it, and it cannot be recovered, so keep the app installed if you want to keep your progress.
