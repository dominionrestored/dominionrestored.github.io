Reporting a problem

To help us track it down quickly, please include:

  • your device (for example, iPhone 15 or Samsung Galaxy S24) and its system version
  • where you were in the game: the land, the dungeon or the mini-game
  • what happened, and what you expected to happen
  • a screenshot or a short screen recording, if you can

Refunds

Purchases are made through the app stores, so refunds are requested from the store you bought the game from:

  • App Store (iPhone, iPad and Mac): reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund help
  • Microsoft Store: request it from your Microsoft account order history
