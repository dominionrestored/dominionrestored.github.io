Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

In short: Dominion Restored does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends anything over the internet. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Dominion Restored is published by Kingdom Management Solutions, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Dominion Restored apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows, and this website.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use your camera or microphone. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and no third-party code that collects data. It makes no network requests while you play.

What stays on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the game stores a few things on your device:

  • your saved game (progress, items and hearts)
  • the look you chose for your hero
  • which mini-game tents you have found, for the Arcade
  • your best score at the shooting range

This information never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the app.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising, and loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses to keep its service secure. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Sharing

Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to share or sell, and we share nothing with anyone.

Children

Dominion Restored is made for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the game has no chat, no accounts and no way to contact other players.

App stores

You download the app through Apple's App Store, Google Play or the Microsoft Store. Those stores may collect information about your purchase and download under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before that change is released.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to Kingdom Management Solutions, LLC at kingdommanagementsolutions@gmail.com.
