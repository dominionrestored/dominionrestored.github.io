Kingdom Management Solutions, LLC is the independent publisher of Dominion Restored.

Why Dominion Restored

In the beginning, God gave mankind dominion over the earth. It was lost in the garden, and the rest of Scripture tells how it is won back. Dominion Restored follows that story from Genesis to Revelation, through the lands, battles and promises of the Bible, and ends where the Bible ends: with all things made new.

"And God blessed them. And God said to them, 'Be fruitful and multiply and fill the earth and subdue it, and have dominion over the fish of the sea and over the birds of the heavens and over every living thing that moves on the earth.'"Genesis 1:28 (ESV)
"And you have made them a kingdom and priests to our God, and they shall reign on the earth."Revelation 5:10 (ESV)
