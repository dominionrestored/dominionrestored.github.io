From Eden to Revelation

What's inside

A big adventure in a small package

10

Dungeons and bosses

Locked doors, keys, treasure rooms and a boss in every land, each fought the way the story tells it.

14

Mini-games

Circus tents across the world hold a game from each story, open from the start. Replay them any time from the Arcade.

KJV

Scripture throughout

Signs, treasures and battles carry verses from the King James Bible, so the story is told in its own words.

2D/3D

Two ways to see it

Play in classic 8-bit 2D, or switch to a 3D diorama of the same pixel art at any time.

+

Weapons of the faith

The Flaming Sword, the Dove, the Rod of Moses, the Shofar, David's sling, the Shield of Faith, the Armor of Light and more.

★

The new earth

Beyond the final battle lies a world made new, full of saints, angels and friendly animals. We'll let you discover it.
